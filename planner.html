Check the creek before anything else

If Arroyo Grande Creek is posted closed, it's closed, no exceptions. Confirm the current creek status the morning you leave, not the night before.

Time your drive to the tide, not the clock

Arrive and depart near low tide for the firmest sand and easiest creek crossing. The plan below will find your exact windows once you enter your dates.

Reservations move fast

Beach camping fills up, especially holiday weekends. Book at ReserveCalifornia or (800) 444-7275 before you plan around a spot.

Confirm fire rules at the gate

Fire restrictions change with conditions. Check current campfire rules so you're not caught out with wood and no fire allowed.
