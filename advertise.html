Yelp charges around $270 a month for an enhanced profile, then bills you $2 to $10 again every time somebody clicks it. Google Ads works the same way, so a busy Saturday costs you more than a slow Tuesday, and a bad month can cost you more than a good one.

We do not do that. You pay one flat monthly rate. If ten thousand people click through to your business, you pay exactly what you would have paid if nobody did. No per-click charge, no bidding against your competitors, no bill that moves around on you.

The Fine Print

What We Will and Will Not Do

  • No pop-ups, no full-screen ads, no video that plays on its own. Nothing that covers the page or blocks what a visitor came to read. The site is useful because it is fast and clean. We are not trading that for ad revenue.
  • Everything is labeled. Sponsored placements say so. Our safety, rules, and emergency information is never for sale, at any price.
  • You keep your creative simple. Send a logo and a phone number. We handle layout so it matches the site and loads fast.
  • 30 days notice to cancel on monthly plans. If you sell or close the business, we refund the months you have not used.
  • The rate is the whole bill. No per-click charges, no setup fee, no design fee, and no bidding against anyone. What is on the card is what you pay.
  • We will turn business away. If a placement would mislead a visitor about park rules or safety, we will say no and explain why.
  • Rates are reviewed once a year. Founding partners are exempt for the length of their lock.
